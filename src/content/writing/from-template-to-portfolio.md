The portfolio started feeling stronger when I stopped writing like any front-end developer could own the page. My background gives me clearer material: Miva software engineering, AI-assisted development, prompt engineering, Pharmacy Technician experience, and HealthTech interest.

That means RxLedger should not be described as just a polished SaaS screen. It should point to branch inventory, FEFO stock control, POS flow, staff roles, and the pharmacy operations problems that made the project worth building.
