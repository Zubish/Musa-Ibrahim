## The goal

This piece explores how a stronger headline, cleaner pacing, and better visual hierarchy can make a landing page feel more convincing from the first screen.

## The approach

I framed the page around a confident hero section, better section pacing, and more readable content blocks. The emphasis is on helping a visitor understand the offer quickly without feeling overwhelmed by decoration.

## Why this piece matters

It shows how I think about persuasion on the web: clearer structure, tighter messaging, and front-end choices that make the page feel more intentional.
