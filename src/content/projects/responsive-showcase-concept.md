## The goal

This piece treats mobile as its own design problem, with content priority and layout flow handled deliberately instead of being compressed from desktop.

## The approach

The design emphasizes flexible layouts, controlled animation, and a clear reading path that keeps the experience lightweight and easy to follow on any device.

## Why this piece matters

It shows how I approach responsive presentation: simplify the reading path, keep motion controlled, and make the layout feel natural at every size.
