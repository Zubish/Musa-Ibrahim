Process

From template to portfolio with personality

A template becomes a real portfolio when the content, hierarchy, and decisions start sounding like the person behind the work.

The shift usually starts with better writing. Project descriptions become more specific, the About section becomes more personal, and calls to action become more confident.

Then design decisions reinforce that voice through stronger typography, clearer project storytelling, and more intentional structure. Personality is usually created through editing, not excess.
